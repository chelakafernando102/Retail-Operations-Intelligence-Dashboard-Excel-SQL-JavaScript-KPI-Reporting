# Executive Summary

## Project: Retail Operations Intelligence Dashboard

This project simulates an analyst workflow for a retail operations team. The goal is to identify sales trends, profitability drivers, stockout risks, fulfillment inefficiencies, and improvement opportunities using structured KPI reporting.

## Findings

1. Electronics produced the strongest revenue, but margin performance was weaker than categories such as Apparel and Health & Beauty.
2. Stockout risk was associated with longer processing times, creating a clear operational bottleneck.
3. Higher discount levels reduced margin performance, suggesting promotional activity should be reviewed more carefully.
4. Regional sales concentration shows where leadership should prioritize inventory availability and fulfillment capacity.
5. Recurring KPI dashboards can improve visibility into sales, profit, stockout risk, and processing efficiency.

## Business Recommendations

- Build reorder-point alerts for categories with repeated stockout risk.
- Review low-margin, high-revenue categories to improve pricing and promotional strategy.
- Track processing time by fulfillment mode to identify workflow delays.
- Use dashboard reporting in weekly operations reviews to improve decision-making.
