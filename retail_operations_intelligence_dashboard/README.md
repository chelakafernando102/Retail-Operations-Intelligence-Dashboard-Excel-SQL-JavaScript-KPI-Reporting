# Retail Operations Intelligence Dashboard

This business analytics portfolio project was built around the operational and customer-facing experience I gained working at Walmart, combined with my technical skills and interest in data analysis. Working in a fast-paced retail environment gave me exposure to inventory flow, merchandising, operational efficiency, and customer trends, which inspired me to explore how data can be used to improve business decisions. This project reflects my interest in Business Analyst, Operations Analyst, and Financial Analyst career paths while showcasing my ability to turn operational data into actionable insights.

## Project Overview

This project is inspired by my experience working in retail operations at Walmart and my interest in analytics-driven roles. It focuses on analyzing sales performance, inventory movement, fulfillment efficiency, profitability, and regional trends to simulate the type of reporting and operational analysis used in real business environments. The goal was to create something that feels like an actual junior analyst project, combining practical business insights, KPI tracking, SQL analysis, and an executive-style dashboard.

## Business Questions

1. Which product categories generate the strongest sales and profit?
2. Where are stockout risks creating operational bottlenecks?
3. How do discounts affect profit margin?
4. Which regions and fulfillment modes perform best?
5. What operational recommendations can improve retail efficiency?

## Analyst Insights

- Electronics generated the highest sales volume but produced a lower margin profile than Apparel and Health & Beauty.
- Stockout risk was concentrated in low-inventory orders where processing time increased by roughly 2–3 hours on average.
- Atlantic and Ontario represented the strongest revenue regions in the sample dataset.
- Discounting above 15% reduced profit margin materially, suggesting tighter promotion controls could improve profitability.
- Pickup and Same Day fulfillment showed faster processing times but should be monitored for inventory accuracy.

## Recommended Actions

- Monitor categories with high revenue but low margins to improve profitability.
- Reduce stockout risk through reorder-point monitoring and better replenishment timing.
- Limit deep discounting where margin erosion outweighs sales lift.
- Track processing time by fulfillment method to improve operational throughput.
- Build recurring KPI reporting for sales, profit, stockout risk, and inventory accuracy.

## Dataset Note

The included dataset is simulated for portfolio demonstration and avoids using private employer data. The dashboard can also be adapted to public retail datasets such as Tableau's Sample Superstore or Kaggle Superstore datasets.
